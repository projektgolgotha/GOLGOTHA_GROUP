How a claim becomes public

Each material claim should carry an owner, source, date, exact scope, review date and status: documented, implemented, tested or externally verified. A published policy is not proof it is enforced; an application is not an award; a completion badge is not automatically transferable academic credit.

Families and academic reviewers can ask ENOCH ONE for public references, or contact the team to request clarification. The Discord library is public within the server. Confidential learner identity, consent, assessments, credentials and incident records must use a separately restricted process; that process and its access controls are not verified by public channel setup.
