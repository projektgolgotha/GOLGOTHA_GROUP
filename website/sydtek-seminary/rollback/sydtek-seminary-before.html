WEB4 + NIL / ENROLLMENT BENEFIT

A family office for every enrolled SydTek student

During enrollment, the Goldston Family Office will launch a family-office program for every enrolled SydTek student. Each student begins with a private, family-controlled workspace for learning, opportunity, and long-term stewardship. For minors, the launch is led by a parent or guardian.

The included launch provides a family charter, goals and budget, a NIL and intellectual-property register, permission tracking, and an income-and-expense record. There is no separate launch fee. Enrollment does not transfer student rights or guarantee earnings.
