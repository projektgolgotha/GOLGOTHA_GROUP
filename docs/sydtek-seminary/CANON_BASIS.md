# Basis of the 81-entry canon registry

**Prepared:** 30 October 2026. **Scope:** a documented curricular numbering basis, not a claim to settle all Ethiopian canonical history or to possess an approved edition of every text.

## Declared inventory

The registry adopts the numbered list published by the [Ethiopian Orthodox Tewahedo Church](https://ethiopianorthodox.org/english/canonical/books.html): **46 Old Testament entries and 35 New Testament entries**. The [Mahibere Kidusan church department](https://eotcmk.org/e/holy-scriptures/) reproduces that inventory and identifies a Holy Synod publication among its sources. The source's ordinal numbers are preserved in stable IDs OT-01–OT-46 and NT-01–NT-35. The registry retains the published English label alongside a normalized classroom title.

Some numbers contain multiple texts; some familiar bodies of text are divided. Therefore a count of 81 registry rows is not a claim of 81 separately bound modern-English books, 81 equal workloads, 81 completed lessons, or 81 academic credits. This is the published 46/35 inventory selected for this project; it should not be mixed silently with another numbering scheme.

## Identity and coverage decisions

| Registry entry | Operational decision |
|---|---|
| OT-09; OT-10; OT-15 | Preserve combined Samuel, Kings, and Ezra/Nehemiah entries; track their component books individually |
| OT-16 | Keep Ezra II and Ezra Sutuel as an unresolved edition crosswalk; Esdras numbering varies |
| OT-20; OT-21 | Map Ethiopic 1 Meqabyan and the grouped 2–3 Meqabyan entry; retain the church source's Maccabees labels as provenance |
| OT-24; OT-25 | Preserve the source's distinction between Proverbs and Reproof |
| OT-30; OT-32 | Require an explicit component list for Jeremiah and Daniel in the adopted edition |
| OT-46 | Seek the Joseph ben Gorion/Josippon text; a generic Josephus resource is contextual until identity is demonstrated |
| NT-28–31 | Map four specific Sinodos units; a general collection title does not establish each part |
| NT-32–33 | Preserve Dominos I/II source labels while verifying the Covenant I/II teaching crosswalk |
| NT-34–35 | Verify Ethiopic Clement and Didascalia identities; similarly titled works in other traditions need comparison |

[Mikre-Sellassie's original study](https://translation.bible/wp-content/uploads/2024/06/mikre-sellassie-1993-the-bible-and-its-canon-in-the-ethiopian-orthodox-church.pdf), pp. 120–123, explains alternative counts and grouped corpora. It discusses Proverbs/Reproof, Jeremiah-related writings, Daniel additions, Sinodos, Covenant, and Joseph ben Gorion. It is contextual scholarship; the adopted row order remains the declared church webpage inventory. A qualified Ethiopic specialist should approve editions and component boundaries before formal coverage is signed off.

United Bible Societies resources on [1 Meqabyan](https://tips.translation.bible/story/the-book-of-1-meqabyan/) and [3 Meqabyan](https://tips.translation.bible/story/the-book-of-3-meqabyan/) distinguish those Ethiopic works from the similarly numbered Greek Maccabees. Keep the user's Meqabyan playlists separate from the Greek Maccabees historical playlist.

## Supplemental study

Book of Giants, Codex Gigas, generic Josephus studies, comparative Quran/Bible material, C. S. Lewis, angelology, demonology, New Atheism, and Mythological Drift can support contextual or elective learning. Their availability does not add rows to this adopted canon. A Dead Sea Scrolls or Septuagint collection is likewise a source collection, not one additional canonical book. A playlist titled Apocrypha must be inspected at work level before mapping.

## Coverage release rule

For each unit, an academic reviewer must confirm the work, edition, component texts, prescribed passage scope, teaching-source match, learning activity, and assessment. Record at least:

- Registry ID and component IDs.
- Translator/editor, edition, publication details, and lawful access route.
- Assigned reading ranges and whether the scope is selected passages or full text.
- Exact video IDs and segments used as teaching resources.
- Outcome and assignment identifiers.
- Reviewer's identity, review date, decision, and open gaps.

The playlist inventory and source map are discovery evidence. No unit becomes academically complete because its title has a match. Learner attainment requires a separate reviewed submission record.
