## 3. Read and analyze

1. Write three specific observations about the passage, each with a reference.
2. Identify its genre and immediate literary context.
3. Explain a theme or argument in 250–350 words using at least two textual details.
4. Distinguish the primary passage from a teaching source's explanation. Where do you agree, differ, or need further evidence?
5. Identify one plausible alternative interpretation or an unresolved question. Explain what would help assess it.
6. Propose a careful application or teaching use in 75–100 words. Explain how it follows from the reading.
