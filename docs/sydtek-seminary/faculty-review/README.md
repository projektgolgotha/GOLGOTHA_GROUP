# SydTek Seminary faculty-review release — SEM-102 through SEM-106

**Release:** FR-OT-01  
**Prepared:** 31 October 2026  
**Status:** Drafts submitted for faculty review; not academically adopted  
**Scope:** Remaining Old Testament survey sequence in the proposed ECS-300 81-book delivery track

## Purpose

This folder converts the existing course outlines for **SEM-102 through SEM-106** into reviewable course proposals. The drafts are designed to let qualified faculty evaluate the curriculum before any assessed cohort, credit assignment, credential claim, or public representation of academic approval.

The files deliberately preserve unresolved text and edition questions. They do not use a familiar title, a playlist title, or an adjacent biblical work as a substitute for a required Ethiopic text. In particular, faculty decisions are still required for:

- Ezra II and Ezra Sutuel;
- Joseph ben Gorion / Josippon;
- Ethiopic 1–3 Meqabyan as texts distinct from Greek Maccabees;
- Reproof / Tegsats and its proposed Proverbs crosswalk;
- Wisdom / Metsihafe Tibeb;
- the component boundaries of the Jeremiah and Daniel corpora;
- the selected primary editions and exact required readings for every unit.

## Faculty-review sequence

1. **Bibliographic review:** approve the primary edition, component boundaries, and required passage list for every registry unit.
2. **Content review:** evaluate accuracy, theological framing, historical claims, balance, and treatment of alternative interpretations.
3. **Curriculum review:** confirm course outcomes, program alignment, sequence, prerequisites, workload, and learner level.
4. **Assessment review:** confirm that each graded task directly measures the stated outcomes and that the rubric can be applied consistently.
5. **Delivery review:** confirm accessibility, learner support, feedback capacity, copyright permissions, and secure academic records.
6. **Recorded decision:** approve, approve with required revisions, return for redesign, or hold pending evidence.

No course is ready for assessed delivery until all required decisions in its **Faculty decisions before adoption** section are resolved and recorded.
