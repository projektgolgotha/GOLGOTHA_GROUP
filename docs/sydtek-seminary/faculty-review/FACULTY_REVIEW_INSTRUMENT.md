## Rating scale

Use this scale for each criterion.

- **0 — Not evidenced:** absent, materially unreliable, or impossible to evaluate.
- **1 — Emerging:** present but consequential gaps remain.
- **2 — Pilot-ready with controls:** sufficient for a bounded noncredit pilot after named corrections.
- **3 — Strong:** coherent, well-supported, teachable, assessable, and appropriately documented.
- **N/A — Outside reviewer scope:** explain and identify the role that must review it.

A numeric total is descriptive only. One unresolved high-risk item can prevent adoption regardless of the total.
