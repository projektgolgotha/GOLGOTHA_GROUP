# SEM-101 — Skool Build Map

**Course card title:** `SEM-101 | Torah and Israel’s Beginnings`  
**Publication state:** Draft until academic review and instructor assignment.

## Course-card description

Study Genesis through Ruth through selected passages, traceable source records, comparative interpretation, and a short teaching demonstration. This is a proposed noncredit Seminary course in development. It is not an accredited course and does not presently carry academic credit.

## Folder and page sequence

### 00 — Start Here
1. **Welcome, status, and learning outcomes** — course scope, noncredit status, prerequisites, workload estimate, and five outcomes.
2. **Materials, edition record, and study rhythm** — how to identify the translation/edition, record sources, use video teaching critically, and disclose AI assistance.
3. **Assessment and revision rules** — eight worksheets, comparative essay, teaching demonstration, study check, discussions, proposed 80% standard, and revision opportunity.

### 01 — Creation, Promise, and Liberation
4. **Genesis close reading** — Genesis 1–3, 12, 15, and 22; observation before interpretation.
5. **Exodus and communal covenant** — Exodus 1–3, 12–14, and 19–20; comparison activity.
6. **Block 1 submission** — Genesis and Exodus worksheets plus 150–200-word discussion response.

### 02 — Holiness, Community, and Wilderness
7. **Leviticus: ritual, holiness, and community** — Leviticus 16, 19, and 25.
8. **Numbers: leadership, complaint, and judgment** — Numbers 11, 13–14, and 27:1–11.
9. **Block 2 submission** — 400–500-word responsible-teaching memo plus two worksheets.

### 03 — Memory, Land, and Covenant Choice
10. **Deuteronomy: memory and decision** — Deuteronomy 6, 10:12–22, 15, and 30.
11. **Joshua: land, leadership, and covenant retelling** — Joshua 1, 2, and 24.
12. **Block 3 submission** — 700–900-word comparative interpretation.

### 04 — Leadership, Vulnerability, and Belonging
13. **Judges: cycles, leadership, and narrative warning** — Judges 2, 4–5, 6, and 21:25.
14. **Ruth: belonging and whole-book reading** — Ruth 1–4.
15. **Block 4 teaching task** — ten-minute lesson, one-page facilitator guide, and revision reflection.

### 05 — Course Check and Portfolio
16. **Ten-question interpretation study check** — publish without the answer key on the same page.
17. **Correction exercise and answer explanations** — unlock after the learner submits the first attempt or publish as a separate instructor-controlled page.
18. **Final portfolio checklist** — eight worksheets, essay, teaching artifact, discussion evidence, revision log, source list, and AI-use disclosure.
