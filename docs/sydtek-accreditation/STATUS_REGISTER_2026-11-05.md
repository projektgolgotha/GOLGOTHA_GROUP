## Institutional decisions remain separate

- **Scholars:** reviewed source status is Cognia pre-application; no official membership/application acceptance, review decision or award was verified.
- **Legal/registration:** Scholars files conflict between an LLC and a proposed Delaware nonstock nonprofit, and between completed and pending formation, registration, EIN and banking. No entity form or completed status is selected without official evidence.
- **University/Seminary:** no accepted accreditation application, award or degree-granting authority verified. Professional and research doctoral ambitions need appropriate program scope and a confirmed route.
- **Operations:** policy adoption, board/faculty appointments, financial controls, restricted record operation and assessed learner outcomes require implementation records.
- **Partners:** prototype creation does not establish district/church adoption. Existing Union Baptist teaching-history links are retained; no new Coastal delivery claim is made.

## Counting and scoring rules

The 58-document library, 118 accreditation posts and 674 catalog/design records overlap and use different units; do not sum them as independent evidence. The 674 register is a dated published inventory, not a verified count of complete courses. Credential definitions remain inactive.

Retain the October 23 historical outline-coverage baseline (Scholars 10/10, University 8/10, Seminary 5/10) until each domain is re-scored against an explicit rubric. Add publication/delivery evidence without silently promoting a domain to fully verified operation. The Seminary workbook's approximately 23.4% weighted planning estimate is a different measure and is not an accreditation percentage.

## Learner evidence and public resources

The Discord publication record establishes public reference access and search checks. It does not establish enrollment, student achievement or tested safeguarding. Confidential identity, consent, assessment, credential and incident records require a separately restricted process with assigned staff and tested access. No private learner data is included in this release.

For a bounded pilot, collect the course/version, responsible instructor, permissions, actual participation, learner artifact, human rubric assessment, feedback, revision and review decision. Use de-identified aggregates for public reports and retain the authorized underlying records privately.
